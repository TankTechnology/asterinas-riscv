## Interpretation

The TCG and U-Boot inputs are not byte-identical, and their address-translation declaration differs (`sv57` versus the Sv39 U-Boot artifact). This is a real handoff difference worth preserving in the startup audit. It is not the KVM silent-panic mechanism: the TCG DTB has `mmu-type`, while the KVM DTB does not. The r58 direct TCG run also reached `Enter riscv_boot`, `OSTD initialized`, and `first-kthread-enter`, so the TCG discrepancy is not a pre-UART zero-CPU panic. The remaining question is whether the Sv57 declaration contributes to the later Sv39/Stage1 stall; that needs a bounded boot comparison, not another Firefox run.
